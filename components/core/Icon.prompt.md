Thin-stroke line icon (Lucide), used for arrows, contact rows, service glyphs and social links.

```jsx
<Icon name="arrow-right" size={16} />
<Icon name="mail" size={16} color="var(--brass-400)" />
```

Always keep `strokeWidth` at 1–1.25: COZABAL icons are hairline, never bold. Load
`https://unpkg.com/lucide@0.470.0/dist/umd/lucide.js` on the page.
